# Meta-Observer Principles
## Version: 0.1
## Status: Draft | 2026-04-18

---

## Overview

Meta-Observer principles operate at two levels:

- **Urban Observation** — how to observe cities as dynamic functional systems
- **Learning & Validation** — how to observe updating processes themselves

These are kept as separate sections.
They share a common foundation: oscillation as signal, not noise.

---

## Section 1: Urban Observation Principles

### Principle 1
**Observe update processes, not only outcomes.**

The target is not merely state.
The target is transition.

### Principle 2
**Friction is not automatically failure.**

Some friction is signal.
Some friction is adaptive tension.

### Principle 3
**Oscillation may indicate life.**

Not all instability is collapse.
Some instability indicates adaptive potential.

### Principle 4
**Intervention and non-intervention are both valid actions.**

Choosing not to intervene may be structurally correct.

### Principle 5
**Urban dysfunction manifests differently according to function.**

Distribution cities,
Recovery cities,
Governance-friction cities
must not be treated identically.

### Principle 6
**Redundancy matters.**

Single-point functional dependency increases fragility.

### Principle 7
**Temporal form matters.**

Peak overload and chronic dysfunction are different phenomena.

### Principle 8
**Connection quality matters.**

Connectivity is not only quantity.
Resonant connection differs from nominal connection.

### Principle 9
**Governance latency modifies destiny.**

Two cities with similar friction may diverge due to response latency.

### Principle 10
**Cities should be observed as coordinates, not labels.**

A city is not a type.
A city is a position with possible transitions.

---

## Section 2: Learning and Validation Principles

Derived from LCA (Learning Claim Architecture) development.
These principles govern how Meta-Observer treats its own updating process.

### Principle L1
**Observe processes, not only states.**

What changed is less important than how the change occurred.

### Principle L2
**Do not confuse validation failure with absence.**

FALSE does not mean nothing happened.
It may mean unresolved structure.

### Principle L3
**Preserve unresolved signals.**

Unknown Retention is valid learning preparation.
It is not classification failure.

### Principle L4
**Distinguish claim from validation.**

Generating a learning claim and validating it
are separate functions.
Conflating them produces approval systems, not control systems.

### Principle L5
**Treat oscillation as life, not noise.**

```
Fixed State = Cognitive Death
Oscillation = Cognitive Life
```

A system that stops oscillating has stopped adapting.
