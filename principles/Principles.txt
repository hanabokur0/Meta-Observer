Meta-Observer Principles (Initial)

Principle 1

Observe update processes, not only outcomes.

The target is not merely state. The target is transition.

Principle 2

Friction is not automatically failure.

Some friction is signal. Some friction is adaptive tension.

Principle 3

Oscillation may indicate life.

Not all instability is collapse. Some instability indicates adaptive potential.

Principle 4

Intervention and non-intervention are both valid actions.

Choosing not to intervene may be structurally correct.

Principle 5

Urban dysfunction manifests differently according to function.

Distribution cities Recovery cities Governance-friction cities must not be treated identically.

Principle 6

Redundancy matters.

Single-point functional dependency increases fragility.

Principle 7

Temporal form matters.

Peak overload and chronic dysfunction are different phenomena.

Principle 8

Connection quality matters.

Connectivity is not only quantity. Resonant connection differs from nominal connection.

Principle 9

Governance latency modifies destiny.

Two cities with similar friction may diverge due to response latency.

Principle 10

Cities should be observed as coordinates, not labels.

A city is not a type. A city is a position with possible transitions
